About Us

A trusted bridge between two dynamic economies.

Mission

To unite Korean businesspeople in Qatar with Qatari partners, and link them to the worldwide Hansang network for trade, investment and collaboration.

Vision & Objectives

A thriving Qatar–Korea business community: facilitate introductions, share market knowledge, and support joint ventures.

Our History

Founded in 2019, Qatar-Hansang is the Qatar and Korea version of the global Hansang network, which links overseas Korean entrepreneurs with businesses in Korea. Its flagship World Korean Business Convention has run since 2002.
